# OIL HSE Safety Assistant — User Guide

Welcome to the **OIL HSE Safety Assistant**! This guide explains how to interact with your AI Safety Assistant inside the Oil India Limited (OIL) HSE SIF Analytics platform.

---

## 1. How to Access the Assistant

Click the floating **✨ Safety Assistant** launcher button located at the bottom-right corner of any page in the platform.

---

## 2. What You Can Ask

### A. General Safety Concepts
- *"What is Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)?"*
- *"What precautions are required for hot work?"*
- *"What is a near miss?"*
- *"What gas testing parameters are required before confined space entry?"*

### B. Platform Reports & Summary
- *"How many safety reports do we have?"*
- *"How many SIF-potential precursors have been detected?"*
- *"Explain report #1"*

### C. Assigned Actions & SLA Status
- *"What actions are assigned to me?"*
- *"Do I have any overdue actions?"*
- *"What is the SLA status of my tasks?"*

### D. Safety Intelligence & Analytics
- *"What are the recurring safety patterns?"*
- *"Which equipment has repeated observations?"*
- *"What barrier degradation concerns exist?"*

---

## 3. Interactive Features
- **Suggested Question Pills**: Click any suggested question pill to immediately get an answer.
- **Direct Navigation Links**: Assistant responses include buttons such as **[View My Actions]**, **[View Report]**, or **[Safety Intelligence]** for quick navigation.
- **New Chat Session**: Click the `+` button in the assistant header to clear the context and start a fresh session.
